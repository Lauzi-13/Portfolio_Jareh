About the Chef

Kitchen-trained. Hygiene-obsessed. Community-rooted.

Terrency Zwabenda Mdluli is a dedicated and hardworking chef with practical experience across professional kitchens and large-scale traditional catering. From flexing on the line at Avani Resort Hotel in Livingstone, to running the kitchen floor at Artelier Camario in Lusaka, to now leading as Head Chef / Sous Chef at Clay Port, he has built a reputation for consistency under pressure, spotless hygiene standards, and food that brings people together.

Since 2017, he has also run a freelance catering practice — feeding hundreds of guests at Lwiindi ceremonies, weddings and family gatherings — while currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Public Health, adding a science-backed edge to his food-safety and sanitation practice.
